      About

      Play chess against the computer, at your level

      Practise against the computer

      Choose an opponent from beginner, who misses simple tactics, through Elo 1400 to 2400 up to full strength. Play White or Black, ask for a hint when you are stuck, and switch on the evaluation or the best move only when you want them. New to chess? How to play explains the rules, with a board to try each one.

      Analyse any position

      The analysis board shows the best move, the evaluation and the top three lines, with arrows on the board. Enter moves for both sides by tapping or by typing them, load a position from FEN, or continue a game from PGN. After a game, the review marks your inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders and shows the better move.

      Free, private, no account

      The engine runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no sign-up. Your games never leave your device. %VITE_APP_NAME% is free and open source.
